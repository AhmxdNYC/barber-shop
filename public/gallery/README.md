# Cut photographs

Drop image files in here and they appear on `/gallery`, sorted by filename.

    01-skin-fade.jpg      ->  "Skin fade"
    02-taper.jpg          ->  "Taper"
    03-beard-shape-up.jpg ->  "Beard shape up"

The number sets the order; the rest becomes the caption. `.jpg`, `.png`,
`.webp` and `.avif` are picked up.

**Only use photographs the shop owns.** Images on a Google Maps listing
belong to whoever uploaded them — often customers, not the business — and
reusing them on the shop's own commercial site is the shop's legal problem,
not the photographer's. Eduardo can export the ones he uploaded himself from
his Google Business Profile, and anything shot in the shop is his to use.

Square-ish crops work best; the grid renders them 1:1. Send full-size
originals rather than pre-shrinking them — detail thrown away cannot be
recovered.
